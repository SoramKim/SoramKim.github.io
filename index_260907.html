Soram Kim

Radar signal processing & sensor fusion

I work on mmWave propagation and radar sensing, and on fusing radar with other perception sensors to overcome the limits of each and make sensing more reliable. I am interested in the full chain, from radar hardware and the physics of the received signal to AI-based applications.
